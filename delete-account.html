Delete your FixPro account

App: FixPro · Developer: Global Nova Technologies

Delete in the app

  1. Open FixPro and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Review what will be deleted, tick the confirmation box, tap Delete my account and enter the code sent to your phone to confirm it is you.

Can't open the app?

Email ahmiikhanx885@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FixPro account" from the email address, or mentioning the phone number, on your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email, city and photo
  • Saved addresses
  • Chats, including photos, shared locations and voice messages
  • Your reviews and booking history
  • For providers: services, portfolio photos, CNIC details and images, and earnings history

What may be kept

Records we are legally required to keep, such as payment records or reports about safety incidents, may be kept for up to the period required by law and then deleted. They are not used for any other purpose.
